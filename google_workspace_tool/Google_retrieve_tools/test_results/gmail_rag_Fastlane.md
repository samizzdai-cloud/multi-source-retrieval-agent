# 五一劳动节放假通知 Holiday Notification of Labor Day

### [第 1 轮] From: Admin <administration@fstln.io> | Date: Wed, 22 Apr 2026 18:00:53 +0800
**To**: "Fastlane.HQ" <Fastlane.HQ@fstln.io>

各位小伙伴们：

根据国务院2026年节假日安排，同时结合公司实际情况，现将我司2026年五一劳动节假期安排如下：

放假时间：2026年5月1日至5月5日，共 5 天。

注：2026年5月9日（星期六）正常上班。

放假注意事项：

1. 放假前，请各部门人员关好门窗、关闭电源及水源等。

2. 出行应注意人身和财产安全。

祝愿大家度过一个愉快、安全、充实的劳动节！

Hi All,

According to the State Council's holiday arrangement in 2026 and the actual
situation of the company, we will have the holiday as follows:

The holiday starts from May 1, 2026, to May 5, 2026. Will need to work on
May 9, 2026.

Holiday Notes:

1. Please do a safety check and hygiene cleaning before the holiday, to
ensure that doors and windows are closed, and the power of electrical
appliances has been turned off.

2. During the holiday period, please pay attention to the property and
personal safety when you go out.

Wishing you all a happy, safe, and fulfilling Labor Day.
